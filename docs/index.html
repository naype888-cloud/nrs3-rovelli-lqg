NRS³ · Rovelli — area quanta and the localization ceiling

In loop quantum gravity (Rovelli, La realidad no es lo que parece, Ch. 6) a surface can only have area A(j) = 8 π γ lp² √(j (j + 1)) with j ∈ ½ ℕ: one puncture gives a ladder of separated levels, and no surface carries a fraction of the lowest one. In NRS³ this granularity is dimensional uncertainty: the NRS angle θ_NRS(d) = arccos(1/C_Nava(d)) of an axis with d sites is 0 for d = 2, 3, 7.43° at d = 4, and grows toward arccos(1/C∞) ≈ 28.30° without ever reaching it.

Scope

Proved here: the LQG area spectrum and puncture counting as stated, and the NRS³ ceiling restated from the base repository. The NRS³ core is the base repository and does not depend on this page. The readings below — granularity as dimensional uncertainty, the graviton at the ceiling — are proposals, not results of NRS³: developing them is work for quantum information and quantum gravity.

In NRS³ (proposal)

1. Granularity = dimensional uncertainty: the granularity of quantum space is read as the dimensional uncertainty of (T_d, P_d), measured by θ_NRS(d): 0 at d = 2, 3, 7.43° at d = 4, strictly below arccos(1/C∞) ≈ 28.30° for every d.
2. Area quanta: each puncture carries at least one lowest quantum A(1/2); the entropy is linear in the number of punctures, as the entropy of a cut in nrs3-defect-curvature is linear in its δ∞ quanta (a reading, not a theorem here).
3. The graviton: the maximally localized excitation has no finite-d state at the ceiling; arccos(1/C∞) is a limit, approached as d → ∞ and never reached.

Source text: Carlo Rovelli, La realidad no es lo que parece (2017), Ch. 5 (quantum space-time), Ch. 6 (area and volume quanta, spin networks) and Ch. 10 (black-hole entropy by counting punctures).
